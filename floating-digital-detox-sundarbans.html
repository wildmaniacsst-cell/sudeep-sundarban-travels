A DIFFERENT KIND OF JOURNEY

Not another itinerary.
A pause.

NirvaanAfloat is built around a simple idea: travel does not always need to be packed with activities. Sometimes the most valuable part of a journey is creating space to notice what is around you, and what has been missing from your everyday routine.

On a boat moving through the tidal landscape, the river becomes your clock. Mangrove edges, shifting light, birds calling and long stretches without a planned distraction invite you to slow down.

It is not about forcing silence or banning technology. It is about choosing when to put the screen away and be present.

HONEST EXPECTATIONS

Nature is the host.
We follow its rules.

The Sundarbans is a living, tidal wilderness, not a controlled wellness resort. Routes and activities may change with tides, weather, safety and local conditions. Wildlife sightings are never promised.

Mobile network and internet availability vary by location and provider. NirvaanAfloat does not promise a signal-free zone; the digital detox is a voluntary choice to reduce screen use during your journey.

Yoga, meditation, meals, accommodation, boat facilities, guides and other inclusions must be confirmed for your chosen dates and itinerary. This is a nature-based travel experience, not a medical or mental-health treatment programme.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you disconnect.

What exactly is NirvaanAfloat?

A floating digital-detox concept in the Sundarbans focused on slower travel, screen-free time and reconnecting with the river and mangrove surroundings.

Will there be no mobile signal or Wi-Fi?

Connectivity varies across the region. We do not guarantee zero signal. Guests are encouraged to switch off or limit devices by choice.

Can yoga or meditation be included?

Optional mindful activities can be discussed when planning. Exact inclusions, availability and space onboard depend on the final itinerary.

Is it suitable for families?

Suitability depends on ages, comfort with boat travel and the agreed itinerary. Discuss your group’s needs before booking.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothing, sun protection, personal medicines, a reusable water bottle if appropriate, and a book or journal. Keep devices available for safety and essential communication.

How do I plan or book?

Send an enquiry with your preferred dates, group size and expectations. We will discuss the boat, route, facilities and activities before confirming details.

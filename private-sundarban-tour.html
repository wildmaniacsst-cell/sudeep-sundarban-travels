Explore Through the Waterways

A Journey Shaped by the River

The waterways are an essential part of exploring the Sundarbans. A boat-based journey allows you to experience the changing landscape, forest edges, creeks and tidal surroundings.

Depending on the trip arrangements, boat experience, itinerary and facilities can be discussed during the planning stage. The journey should be designed around the group's requirements and the realities of operating in a protected mangrove environment.

The forest sets the rules. Our itinerary must respect them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Planning a Private Sundarban Tour

What is a private Sundarban tour?

A private tour is planned for your own group rather than joining a shared group of unrelated travellers. The itinerary and arrangements can be discussed based on your preferences.

Can I customise the itinerary?

Yes. Your travel interests, duration, group size, preferred activities and requirements can be discussed while designing the itinerary, subject to availability and applicable regulations.

Can I book a private trip for my family?

Yes. Family and small-group trips can be planned around your group's travel requirements and comfort preferences.

Can I plan a private photography expedition?

Yes. If wildlife photography is your priority, discuss your photographic interests, preferred trip duration and expectations while planning the journey.

How much does a private Sundarban tour cost?

The price depends on the number of travellers, duration, accommodation, boat arrangements, inclusions and the level of customisation. A specific proposal can be prepared after understanding your requirements.

Are tiger sightings guaranteed on private trips?

No. A private expedition does not guarantee tiger sightings or any specific wildlife encounter. Sightings depend on natural conditions and wildlife behaviour.

How do I enquire about a private expedition?

Contact us with your tentative dates, number of travellers, trip duration and interests. We can discuss the possibilities and available arrangements.

OUR STORY

A lifelong fascination.
Field-led journeys since 2014.

My connection with the Sundarbans began long before tourism became my profession. Growing up in South 24 Parganas, I spent my teenage years exploring riverbanks, mudflats and mangrove edges by bicycle, fascinated by birds, reptiles, mudskippers and the life hidden in this tidal landscape.

After years of weekend journeys, learning from local people, boatmen and forest-dependent communities, I began building wildlife-focused expeditions. On 1 and 12 January 2014, I experienced my first three tiger sightings across two separate trips. That turning point helped shape Sudeep Sundarban Travels into a journey-led, wildlife-first venture.

Today, our expeditions are shaped by field familiarity, local connections, respect for the forest and the people who live beside it. We do not promise tiger sightings. We invite you to experience the Sundarbans with patience, curiosity and a deeper understanding of its wilderness.

Sumit Ghosh Founder & Wildlife Expedition Host
